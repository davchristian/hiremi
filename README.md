# Job Tracker

> A modern, developer-first job software tracker built to organize your internship and full-time softwares in one place.

## Overview

Kanban-style software designed to help students and software engineers keep track of every stage of their job search—from companies on your wishlist to offers and rejections.

Instead of juggling spreadsheets, notes, and emails, Job Tracker provides a clean dashboard that lets you visualize your software pipeline and monitor your progress.
